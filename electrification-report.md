# Electrification Solutions for VAN RYSEL GRVL AF DISCOVER Gravel Bike

## Abstract

This report explores solutions for electrifying the VAN RYSEL GRVL AF DISCOVER, a Microshift Acolyte 1×8 speed gravel bike. The study evaluates three complete electrification kits considering budget constraints (€3,000), regulatory compliance (European road standards), weight limitations (<30 kg total), and performance requirements (100 km range, <3h charging). A critical analysis compares hub motor and mid-drive systems, leading to a recommended solution optimized for mixed city and off-road use.

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Requirements and Limitations

The VAN RYSEL GRVL AF DISCOVER is a versatile gravel bike equipped with a Microshift Acolyte 1×8 speed drivetrain, weighing approximately 12 kg. The electrification project aims to transform this bike into a dual-mode electric vehicle suitable for both urban commuting and off-road adventures.

**Technical Specifications of Base Bike:**
- Model: VAN RYSEL GRVL AF DISCOVER
- Drivetrain: Microshift Acolyte 1×8 speed
- Base Weight: ~12 kg
- Rear Hub: Standard 135×9 mm spacing

**Key Requirements:**

*Operating Modes:*
- **R (Road) Mode:** European-compliant assistance up to 25 km/h
- **OR (Off-Road) Mode:** Maximum power output for unrestricted terrain

*Performance Criteria:*
- Range: 100 km per charge
- Charging Time: < 3 hours
- Total Weight: < 30 kg (maintaining rideability without assistance)
- Energy Consumption: 10-15 Wh/km estimated
- Total Energy Budget: 1,000-1,500 Wh for 100 km range

*Installation & Budget:*
- Maximum Budget: €3,000
- Second-hand/refurbished components accepted
- Easy installation without specialist tools preferred
- Preservation of existing drivetrain desirable

### 1.2 Use Case Analysis

The bike will serve mixed purposes:
- **Urban/City:** Daily commuting, efficient road travel
- **Off-Road:** Gravel paths, trails, challenging terrain

This dual-use scenario requires a balanced solution that doesn't compromise the bike's gravel-specific characteristics while adding reliable electric assistance.

## 2. Solution Candidates

### 2.1 Candidate 1: Rear Hub Motor Kit (Budget Option)

**System Components:**

*Motor:*
- Brand: Bafang
- Model: G020 Rear Hub Motor
- Power: 750W (48V)
- Type: Direct drive or geared hub
- Installation: 135×9 mm rear wheel replacement

*Battery:*
- Configuration: Dual 48V 15Ah packs (total 1,440 Wh)
- Capacity: 720 Wh per pack
- Mounting: Frame-mounted (triangle bag style or rear rack)
- Chemistry: Lithium-ion (Samsung/LG cells)

*Electronics & Controls:*
- Controller: 48V 25A smart controller with dual-mode capability
- Display: LCD display with mode switching (R/OR)
- Throttle: Optional twist throttle
- PAS: Pedal Assist Sensor (8-level)

**Pricing:**
- Motor Kit (wheel, motor, controller): €600-800
- Battery Pack (dual setup): €700-900
- Display & Accessories: €100-150
- **Total Estimated Cost:** €1,400-1,850

**Critical Analysis:**

*Advantages:*
- Simple installation: wheel swap with minimal technical skill
- Preserves 1×8 drivetrain completely
- Lower mechanical stress on chain and components
- Cost-effective solution within budget
- Dual battery allows weight reduction for city use
- Easy battery replacement and upgrade path

*Disadvantages:*
- Increased unsprung weight affects handling
- Less efficient on steep climbs
- Center of gravity shifted rearward
- Potential torque arm requirements for safety
- Less natural pedaling feel
- Wider rear dropout may affect clearance

### 2.2 Candidate 2: Mid-Drive Motor Kit (Performance Option)

**System Components:**

*Motor:*
- Brand: Bafang
- Model: BBSHD 1000W
- Power: 1000W (48V/52V compatible)
- Type: Bottom bracket mid-drive
- Installation: Replaces bottom bracket (68-73mm compatible)

*Battery:*
- Configuration: Dual 52V 14Ah packs (total 1,456 Wh)
- Capacity: 728 Wh per pack
- Mounting: Downtube mount + optional secondary
- Chemistry: Lithium-ion (high discharge rate cells)

*Electronics & Controls:*
- Controller: Integrated 30A controller with programmable modes
- Display: Color DPC-18 display
- Programming: USB interface for mode customization
- Sensors: Torque and cadence sensing

**Pricing:**
- Motor Kit (BBSHD complete): €900-1,200
- Battery System (dual 52V): €900-1,100
- Installation Components: €150-250
- **Total Estimated Cost:** €1,950-2,550

**Critical Analysis:**

*Advantages:*
- Optimal weight distribution (center of gravity)
- Superior climbing performance
- More natural pedaling dynamics
- Better off-road handling and traction
- Efficient power transfer
- High power capability for OR mode

*Disadvantages:*
- Increased drivetrain wear (chain, cassette)
- More complex installation
- Requires bottom bracket compatibility check
- Higher component replacement costs over time
- May need chain guard for safety
- Potential chainline issues with 1×8 setup

### 2.3 Candidate 3: Lightweight Hub Motor Kit (Balanced Option)

**System Components:**

*Motor:*
- Brand: Shengyi
- Model: DGW07 250W-500W Geared Hub
- Power: 500W nominal (48V, can peak higher)
- Type: Geared hub motor (lighter than direct drive)
- Installation: 135×9 mm rear wheel replacement

*Battery:*
- Configuration: Single modular 48V 21Ah (1,008 Wh) + optional 48V 10.5Ah (504 Wh)
- Primary Capacity: 1,008 Wh
- Extended Range: 1,512 Wh with second battery
- Mounting: Frame bag (primary) + top tube bag (secondary)
- Chemistry: Lithium-ion (balanced cells)

*Electronics & Controls:*
- Controller: 48V 18A intelligent controller
- Display: Compact LCD with Bluetooth connectivity
- App Integration: Smartphone app for mode management
- PAS: 12-magnet pedal sensor

**Pricing:**
- Motor Kit (complete): €500-700
- Primary Battery: €500-650
- Optional Secondary Battery: €300-400
- Display & Electronics: €150-200
- **Total Estimated Cost:** €1,150-1,550 (base) / €1,450-1,950 (extended)

**Critical Analysis:**

*Advantages:*
- Excellent power-to-weight ratio
- Modular battery system offers flexibility
- EU-compliant 250W rating with unlock capability
- Simpler than mid-drive, lighter than DD hub
- Good balance for mixed use
- Bluetooth connectivity for easy mode switching
- Lower installation complexity

*Disadvantages:*
- Geared hubs require eventual maintenance
- Less power than BBSHD for extreme off-road
- Still adds rear weight (though less than DD)
- May struggle on very steep grades with full load
- Battery mounting may affect aesthetics

## 4. Recommended Solution

### 4.1 Final Selection: Candidate 3 (Lightweight Geared Hub Motor Kit)

After comprehensive analysis, **Candidate 3** is recommended as the optimal solution for electrifying the VAN RYSEL GRVL AF DISCOVER gravel bike.

**Justification:**

1. **Weight Optimization:** At 19-20 kg total, it remains closest to the <30 kg requirement while maintaining rideability without assistance.

2. **Cost-Effectiveness:** Mid-range pricing (€1,450-1,950) provides excellent value while staying well within the €3,000 budget, allowing for quality components and potential upgrades.

3. **Versatility:** The geared hub motor offers a superior balance between city efficiency and off-road capability, matching the mixed-use requirement.

4. **Installation Simplicity:** Straightforward wheel swap without complex drivetrain modifications, making it accessible for self-installation.

5. **Drivetrain Preservation:** The 1×8 Microshift Acolyte system remains untouched, avoiding increased wear and maintaining original bike characteristics.

6. **Modular Flexibility:** The optional secondary battery allows customization based on use case:
   - City commuting: Single battery (lighter, 19 kg total)
   - Long tours: Dual batteries (extended range, 20 kg total)

7. **Smart Features:** Bluetooth integration and app control simplify mode switching between R (road-legal) and OR (off-road) modes.

8. **Compliance & Performance:** 
   - Road mode: 250W nominal meets EU regulations, 25 km/h limit
   - Off-road mode: Higher power output available when unrestricted

### 4.2 Implementation Specifications

**Complete System Configuration:**

- **Motor:** Shengyi DGW07 500W geared hub (48V)
- **Primary Battery:** 48V 21Ah (1,008 Wh)
- **Optional Battery:** 48V 10.5Ah (504 Wh)
- **Controller:** 48V 18A with dual-mode programming
- **Display:** LCD with Bluetooth + smartphone app
- **Sensors:** 12-magnet PAS, speed sensor
- **Mounting:** Frame bag (primary), top tube bag (optional)

**Installation Requirements:**
- Replace rear wheel with motor wheel
- Mount controller and wiring harness
- Install battery bags with secure fastening
- Connect display to handlebars
- Install PAS sensor on crank
- Configure R/OR mode parameters via app

**Expected Performance:**
- Road Mode: 25 km/h assistance, ~120 km range
- Off-Road Mode: ~35-40 km/h capable, ~80-100 km range
- Charging: 2-2.5 hours with 0.5C charger
- Total System Weight: 7-8 kg addition

## 5. Evaluation vs. Ready-Made Electric Bikes

### 5.1 Comparable Market Options

**Ready-Made Electric Gravel Bikes in Similar Class:**

1. **Specialized Turbo Creo SL** (€7,000-9,000)
   - 250W motor, integrated battery
   - Premium but 2-3× over budget

2. **Canyon Grail:ON** (€4,500-5,500)
   - Bosch Performance CX motor
   - Exceeds budget by €1,500-2,500

3. **Decathlon Riverside 540 E** (€2,500-3,000)
   - 250W hub motor, basic components
   - City-oriented, less off-road capable

### 5.2 Comparative Analysis

**Advantages of DIY Electrification:**

*Financial:*
- Cost savings: €1,500-2,000 compared to equivalent ready-made
- Existing bike investment preserved
- Component-level upgrades possible
- Repair flexibility with standard parts

*Customization:*
- Dual-mode capability (R/OR) often unavailable in commercial bikes
- Power output customizable beyond legal limits for off-road
- Battery system tailored to specific range needs
- Ability to choose quality components (Samsung/LG cells)

*Performance:*
- Maintains lightweight geometry of quality gravel frame
- Proven drivetrain (Microshift Acolyte) already familiar
- Customizable power profiles
- Modular battery system for weight optimization

**Disadvantages of DIY Electrification:**

*Integration:*
- Less elegant aesthetic than integrated systems
- Visible batteries and wiring
- Non-seamless controls compared to factory integration

*Support:*
- No manufacturer warranty for complete system
- Self-service maintenance and troubleshooting
- Component compatibility responsibility

*Reliability:*
- Aftermarket quality variance
- Potential electrical integration issues
- No professional pre-delivery inspection

### 5.3 Value Proposition

The DIY electrification approach offers:
- **50-60% cost savings** compared to equivalent ready-made bikes
- **Superior customization** for specific use cases (R/OR modes)
- **Preservation** of a known, quality base platform
- **Upgrade path** for future improvements

Trade-offs include aesthetic integration and warranty support, which are acceptable given the budget constraints and performance requirements.

## 6. Conclusion

The electrification of the VAN RYSEL GRVL AF DISCOVER gravel bike represents a viable and cost-effective approach to creating a dual-purpose electric bicycle suitable for both urban commuting and off-road adventures. 

**Key Findings:**

1. **Technical Feasibility:** All three investigated solutions meet the core requirements of 100 km range, <3h charging, and <30 kg total weight.

2. **Optimal Solution:** The lightweight geared hub motor system (Candidate 3) provides the best balance of performance, cost, weight, and installation simplicity.

3. **Cost Advantage:** At €1,450-1,950, the recommended solution delivers significant savings (€1,500-2,000) compared to equivalent ready-made electric gravel bikes while meeting or exceeding performance specifications.

4. **Dual-Mode Capability:** The implementation of R (road-legal, 25 km/h) and OR (off-road, unrestricted) modes addresses both regulatory compliance and performance requirements effectively.

5. **Modular Design:** The dual-battery option provides flexibility for weight optimization based on use case, maintaining the bike's rideability without assistance.

**Recommendations for Implementation:**

- Prioritize quality battery cells (Samsung/LG) for longevity and safety
- Implement proper battery mounting with secure fastening systems
- Configure road mode conservatively to ensure EU compliance
- Consider waterproofing for electrical connections for off-road use
- Establish maintenance schedule for geared hub motor (~3,000 km intervals)
- Keep detailed records of component sources for future service

**Future Considerations:**

- Battery technology evolution may enable further weight reduction
- Controller firmware updates can optimize efficiency
- Secondary battery addition remains an option if longer range needed
- Component-level upgrades possible without system replacement

The proposed electrification solution successfully transforms the VAN RYSEL GRVL AF DISCOVER into a versatile electric gravel bike while respecting budget constraints, regulatory requirements, and performance expectations. The modular approach ensures adaptability to evolving needs while maintaining the core characteristics that make gravel bikes appealing for mixed-terrain cycling.
